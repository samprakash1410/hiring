>>SDLC 7 STAGES IN HR:
Planning → Requirements → Design → Development → Testing → Deployment → Maintenance

Planning:
 Understand the project requirements, goals, budget, and resources.
 
Requirements:
 Gather and analyze what the software needs to do.
 
Design:
 Create the software architecture, database design, user interface, etc.

Development:
 Developers write the actual code and build the software.

Testing:
 Test the software to identify and fix bugs or defects.

Deployment:
 The completed software is released and made available to users.

Maintenance:
 Monitor the software, fix issues, and make improvements after release.

Market study:
 researching external economic and talent conditions to guide hiring decisions.

>>>Product Life Cycle:
 Introduction → Growth → Maturity → Decline 

 Introduction:
    New recruitment process, new job role, or new employer brand is introduced.

 Growth: 
    Demand for employees increases and the organization expands.

 Maturity:
    Recruitment becomes stable and competition for talent is high.

 Decline:
   Demand for a particular skill/job decreases or the recruitment method becomes outdated.

>>>>Stages of the Employee Life Cycle:
   Attraction → Recruitment → Onboarding → Development → Retention → Separation

   Attraction:
    Creating interest in the organization.(Employer branding, social media, job advertisements, campus recruitment)
   
   Recruitment:
    Finding and selecting suitable candidates.(Sourcing, screening, interviews, assessments, selection)

   Onboarding:
    Helping the new employee settle into the organization.(Orientation, documentation, training, introduction to team)

   Development:
    Improving employee skills and career growth.(Training, mentoring, performance reviews, promotions)

   Retention:
    Keeping valuable employees in the organization.( career opportunities, employee engagement)

   Separation:
    Employee leaves the organization.(Resignation/termination process,)
