//sap//

>Sap:(stands for system application and product in data processing)
  sap is a business software system (ERP) that helps companies manage their different departments in one integrated system.

SAP in HR for HR and recruitment, commonly used SAP solutions include.

>>SAP SuccessFactors:
      recruitment, employee information, onboarding, performance, learning, etc.

>>>SAP HCM (Human Capital Management) 
      traditional SAP HR functions such as personnel administration, payroll, time management, and organizational management.

tools:
  sap,oracle,jd edwards,ms dynamics 365


Modules:
 SAP Functional:
     functional consultants understand how the business works and configure SAP to support it.
       
       ex:SAP FI, CO, MM, SD, HCM, SuccessFactors.


 SAP Technical:
      technical professionals work on the technology behind SAP, including development and system operations.
         
          ex:SAP ABAP, SAP Basis, SAP HANA, SAP Fiori/UI5.

 SAP Middleware / Integration:
         integration connects SAP to external systems.
                     
             ex:SAP Integration Suite, SAP CPI, APIs, IDocs,OData.
