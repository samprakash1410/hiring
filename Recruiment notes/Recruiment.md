pay roll of hr:
  the structure of process of calculate emp compensation, managing deductions, and distributing accurate wages on time while complying with legal regulations.

  /type of payroll /

  --Gross Pay: Total earnings before any deductions, including base salary, overtime, bonuses, and allowances.

  --Deductions: Mandatory or voluntary subtractions from gross pay, including income taxes, social security, provident fund contributions, and health insurance.

  --Net Pay: The final take-home pay an employee receives after all deductions are subtracted 
     (Net Pay = Gross Pay - Deductions).

  --Statutory Compliance: Adhering to labor and tax laws by filing reports and remitting withheld taxes and government dues.

  ..process..

   Data Collection>>Gather attendance, hours worked, leaves, and new hire details.

   Calculation>>Compute gross wages, apply tax withholdings, and determine net pay.

   Disbursement>>Transfer funds to employee bank accounts and issue payslips.

   Record Keeping>>Store financial and tax records securely for audits.
