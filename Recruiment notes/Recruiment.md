Recruiment > the overall process of identify-attracting-screening-shortlisting are hiring quilified candidates for job with-in a organizaion.
 
 >>stages in processing in recruiment<<
  1> Job Description: a formal document that outlines the duties, responsibilities, required skills, and qualifications for a specific role within an organization.
 
   2> sourcing: is a finding a revelent candidate for job opening in the company and figure out what skills and qualites u need in a candidate for a job.

    3> screening: in recruitment is the initial step in the hiring process where recruiters or hiring managers evaluate job applications to determine which candidates are most qualified for the position.

     4> sharing: in this process the revelant candidate deatils send to team lead. so candidate cv is attached along with the company tracker.

     *type of hiring*

     *full time : This is the traditional model where employees work for a company on a permanent basis. this process  we have bench concept .The job is secure to some extends.

     *contract hiring :  hired for specific projects or tasks on a temporary basis.They are typically paid for their services on a project-by-project.in this process  we dont have bench process , the job is not secure.
